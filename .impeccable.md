## Design Context

### Users
Everyday Android phone owners who want to use their phone as a wired webcam for calls, streaming, or recording. They need a quick, dependable connection flow with clear guidance when USB debugging is not ready.

### Brand Personality
Clean, friendly, reassuring. The interface should make an otherwise technical ADB connection feel approachable and predictable.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, consumer-product control surface with warm off-white foundations, leafy green status color, generous breathing room, and a single highly legible live-view focus. Avoid hacker-terminal aesthetics and generic dark neon dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. Make connection state instantly understandable.
2. Keep the live camera view visually primary.
3. Reveal advanced camera settings only when useful.
4. Use plain language and recovery actions for every failure state.
5. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain keyboard-accessible controls.
